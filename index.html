      FAQ

      Before you hop in.

      Short answers to what people ask most. Still unsure? Start with a launch that has no first buy, so it only costs gas.

      What is Nyxen?

      Nyxen is an independent site for creating tokens on Base. It uses the Clanker v5 contracts, so every token is a standard Clanker token that shows up on Clanker, DEX screeners and wallets.

      How much does it cost?

      The Base network fee, usually a few cents, plus any first buy you choose. If Clanker charges a launch fee, your wallet shows it before you sign.

      Where do the trading fees go?

      You set the buy and sell tax, from 1% to 10%. Clanker keeps its protocol share (0.5% of the trade at low taxes, up to 1% at 10%). You choose where the rest goes: to you as the creator, to holders as dividends, to a token burn, or a mix. Creator fees can be claimed on the Fees page.

      Who controls the token?

      The wallet that creates it. Nyxen never holds your keys, your ETH or your tokens.

      Can someone pull the liquidity?

      No. Clanker v5 puts the full supply into a Uniswap v4 pool at launch and the protocol keeps it there.

      What do I need?

      A wallet such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet with a little ETH on Base. On a phone, open this site inside your wallet app's browser.

      Is this financial advice?

      No. New tokens are extremely risky and most lose their value. Only use money you can afford to lose.
